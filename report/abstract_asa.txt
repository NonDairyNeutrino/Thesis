Title: Using Distributed GPU-Based Parallel-in-Time Integration to Model Acoustics in Expanding Volumes

Abstract: 
A Fourier expansion can be applied onto the wave equation to transform it 
from a PDE in space and time to a system of ODEs in wavenumber and time.
As each of these ODEs are independent in wavenumber, they can be solved in parallel.
While these ODEs are traditionally unable to be parallelized in time, the Parareal method
for parallel-in-time integration can be used to potentially gain massive computational speed-ups.
